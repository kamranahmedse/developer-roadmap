# Strings and Dates

Text and date or time values are two of the most common sources of messy, inconsistently formatted data in real datasets. Getting comfortable handling both pays off across nearly every other part of analysis.

Visit the following resources to learn more:

- [@article@Strings in R](https://r4ds.had.co.nz/strings.html)
- [@article@A Comprehensive Introduction to Handling Date & Time in R](https://www.r-bloggers.com/2020/04/a-comprehensive-introduction-to-handling-date-time-in-r/)

- [@video@Difference Between Dates in R](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rH8l2FOGUbM)
