# Feature Creep

Feature creep (also known as scope creep or feature bloat) is the gradual expansion of a product's feature set beyond its original scope, often leading to complexity, reduced usability, and delayed delivery. It occurs when new features are added without proper prioritization or when stakeholder requests are accepted without evaluating their impact. Product Managers must actively guard against feature creep by maintaining a clear product vision, using prioritization frameworks, and saying no to features that don't align with strategic goals. The best products are often those that do fewer things exceptionally well rather than many things adequately.
