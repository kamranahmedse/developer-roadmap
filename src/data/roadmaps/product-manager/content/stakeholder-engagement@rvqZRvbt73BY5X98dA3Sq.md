# Stakeholder Engagement

Stakeholder engagement is the ongoing process of building and maintaining relationships with stakeholders to ensure their support and input throughout the product lifecycle. It involves regular communication, involving stakeholders in appropriate decisions, addressing concerns proactively, and demonstrating how stakeholder input influences product decisions. Effective engagement builds trust, reduces resistance to change, and creates advocates for the product. Product Managers use various engagement techniques including regular updates, demos, feedback sessions, and collaborative planning to keep stakeholders informed and invested.
