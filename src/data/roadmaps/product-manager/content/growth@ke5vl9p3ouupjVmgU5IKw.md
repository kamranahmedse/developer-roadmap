# Growth

The growth stage of the product lifecycle is where the product gains traction in the market and sales begin to increase rapidly. During this phase, the product starts to generate profit, competition increases, and the market expands. Product Managers focus on scaling the product, expanding to new market segments, and differentiating from competitors. Key activities include optimizing the user experience, building partnerships, and investing in features that drive retention and acquisition. The growth stage requires careful balancing of rapid scaling with maintaining product quality and customer satisfaction.
