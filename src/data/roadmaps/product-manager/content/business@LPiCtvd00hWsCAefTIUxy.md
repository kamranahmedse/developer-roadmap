# Business

Business acumen in product management refers to the ability to understand and apply business principles to product decisions. This includes understanding financial metrics, market dynamics, competitive positioning, pricing strategies, and organizational politics. Product Managers with strong business skills can articulate the business case for product initiatives, make trade-off decisions that balance user value with business viability, and communicate effectively with executives and business stakeholders. Business understanding helps PMs prioritize features that drive revenue, reduce costs, or create strategic advantage.
