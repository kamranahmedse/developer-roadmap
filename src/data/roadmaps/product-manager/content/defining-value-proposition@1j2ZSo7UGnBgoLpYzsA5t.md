# Defining Value Proposition

Defining a value proposition is the process of articulating the unique value that a product delivers to its target customers. This involves deep customer research to understand their needs, pains, and desired outcomes, then mapping how the product addresses these. Product Managers work through frameworks like the Value Proposition Canvas to systematically define and refine the proposition. The process includes identifying customer segments, understanding their jobs-to-be-done, mapping pain points and gains, and aligning product features that address these. A well-defined value proposition serves as the north star for product development and go-to-market strategy.
