# Brainstorming Techniques

Brainstorming techniques are structured methods for generating creative ideas and solutions in a group setting. Beyond traditional open brainstorming, Product Managers can leverage various techniques such as mind mapping, SCAMPER, brainwriting, reverse brainstorming, and the 6-3-5 method. Each technique has its strengths and is suited to different contexts - some excel at generating large volumes of ideas, while others are better for refining existing concepts or solving specific problems. Effective brainstorming requires creating a safe environment where all ideas are welcomed, building on others' contributions, and deferring judgment during the ideation phase.
