# Solved Constraints

Solved constraints refer to the limitations and challenges that have been successfully addressed or overcome in the product development process. Understanding which constraints have been solved helps Product Managers assess progress, allocate resources effectively, and identify remaining challenges. Constraints can be technical (feasibility), business (budget, timeline), or user-related (usability, adoption). Documenting solved constraints provides valuable context for future decisions and helps teams build on past successes rather than rediscovering solutions.
