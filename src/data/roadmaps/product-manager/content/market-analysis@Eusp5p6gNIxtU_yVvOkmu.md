# Market Analysis

Market analysis is a critical activity for Product Managers that involves researching and understanding the market in which a product will compete. It encompasses evaluating market size, growth potential, customer segments, competitive landscape, and industry trends. Through market analysis, Product Managers identify opportunities and threats, understand customer needs and behaviors, and make informed decisions about product positioning and strategy. This research forms the foundation for product planning, go-to-market strategies, and investment decisions. Effective market analysis combines both quantitative data (market size, growth rates) and qualitative insights (customer pain points, competitive dynamics).
