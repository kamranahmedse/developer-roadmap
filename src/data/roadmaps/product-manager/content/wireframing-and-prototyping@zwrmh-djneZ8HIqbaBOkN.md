# Wireframing and Prototyping

Wireframing and prototyping are essential design activities that help teams visualize and test product concepts before investing in development. Wireframes are low-fidelity representations of the product's layout and structure, focusing on content placement and functionality rather than visual design. Prototypes are interactive mockups that simulate the user experience, allowing teams to test interactions and gather feedback. Product Managers use wireframes and prototypes to communicate requirements, validate assumptions, and iterate on designs before committing development resources. These tools bridge the gap between abstract requirements and concrete implementations.
