# Competitive Advantage

Competitive advantage is the unique set of qualities or capabilities that allows a product or company to outperform its rivals. It can stem from various sources including superior technology, brand recognition, cost efficiency, network effects, or customer relationships. Product Managers must identify, build, and defend competitive advantages to ensure long-term product success. Sustainable competitive advantages are difficult for competitors to replicate and create lasting value for customers. Understanding your competitive advantage helps guide product development priorities, marketing strategy, and investment decisions.
