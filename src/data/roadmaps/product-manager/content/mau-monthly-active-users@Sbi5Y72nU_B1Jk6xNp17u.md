# MAU (Monthly Active Users)

MAU (Monthly Active Users) measures the number of unique users who engage with a product within a 30-day period. It provides a broader view of the product's reach compared to DAU and is commonly used to track growth over time. Product Managers use MAU to understand the total addressable user base, measure market penetration, and calculate key ratios like DAU/MAU (stickiness) and LTV/CAC (unit economics). MAU is particularly important for subscription and ad-supported business models where the user base size directly impacts revenue potential.
