# Trend

A trend in product management refers to the general direction in which a metric or market is moving over time. Understanding trends helps Product Managers make informed decisions about product direction, resource allocation, and strategic priorities. Trends can be observed in user behavior, market dynamics, technology adoption, competitive landscape, and business metrics. Product Managers analyze trends to anticipate changes, identify opportunities, and proactively adjust their product strategy. Trend analysis requires looking at data over meaningful time periods and distinguishing between short-term fluctuations and genuine directional changes.
