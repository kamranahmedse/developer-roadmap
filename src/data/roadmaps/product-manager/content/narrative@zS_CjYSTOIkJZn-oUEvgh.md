# Narrative

Product narrative is the cohesive story that connects the product's purpose, vision, and value to stakeholders including customers, investors, and team members. A compelling narrative helps teams understand why the product exists, what problems it solves, and where it's heading. For Product Managers, crafting a strong narrative is essential for building alignment, securing resources, and inspiring teams. The narrative should be consistent across all communications - from product roadmaps to investor pitches to marketing materials. It provides context for decisions and helps stakeholders connect their work to the larger product vision.
