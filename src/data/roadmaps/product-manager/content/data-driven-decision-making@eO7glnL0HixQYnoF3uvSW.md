# Data-Driven Decision Making

Data-driven decision making is the practice of basing product decisions on data analysis rather than intuition or opinion alone. It involves collecting relevant data, analyzing it to extract insights, and using those insights to inform product strategy, prioritization, and optimization. For Product Managers, data-driven decision making means establishing metrics frameworks, running experiments (like A/B tests), analyzing user behavior, and measuring outcomes. While data should inform decisions, the best Product Managers combine data with user empathy, strategic thinking, and domain expertise to make well-rounded decisions.
