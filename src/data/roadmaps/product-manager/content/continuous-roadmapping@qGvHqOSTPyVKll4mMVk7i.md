# Continuous Roadmapping

Continuous roadmapping is an approach to product planning that treats the roadmap as a living document rather than a fixed plan. It involves regularly reviewing and updating the roadmap based on new learnings, changing market conditions, and evolving business priorities. This approach embraces uncertainty and change, allowing teams to adapt quickly while maintaining strategic direction. Product Managers practicing continuous roadmapping hold regular roadmap reviews, incorporate feedback from stakeholders and customers, and adjust priorities based on real data. This keeps the roadmap relevant and aligned with current realities.
