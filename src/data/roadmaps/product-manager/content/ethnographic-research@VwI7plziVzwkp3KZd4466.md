# Ethnographic Research

Ethnographic research is a qualitative research method that involves observing and interacting with users in their natural environment to understand their behaviors, needs, and pain points in context. Unlike interviews or surveys that rely on what users say, ethnography reveals what users actually do, often uncovering needs and behaviors that users themselves may not be aware of or able to articulate. For Product Managers, ethnographic research provides deep, contextual insights that can lead to breakthrough product innovations. Methods include contextual inquiry, diary studies, and shadowing. This approach is particularly valuable during product discovery when teams need to understand complex user workflows and environmental factors.
