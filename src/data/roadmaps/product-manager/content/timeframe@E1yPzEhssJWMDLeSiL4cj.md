# Timeframe

A timeframe in product management is the defined period within which specific goals, initiatives, or milestones are expected to be achieved. Setting appropriate timeframes is critical for planning, prioritization, and accountability. Product Managers work with various timeframes - from sprint-level (1-4 weeks) to quarterly OKRs to annual strategic plans to multi-year product visions. The right timeframe depends on the nature of the goal, market dynamics, and organizational context. Well-defined timeframes create urgency, enable resource planning, and provide clear checkpoints for measuring progress and making adjustments.
