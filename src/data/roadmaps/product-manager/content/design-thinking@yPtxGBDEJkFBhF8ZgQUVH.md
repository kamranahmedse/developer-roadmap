# Design Thinking

Design thinking is a human-centered approach to innovation and problem-solving that integrates the needs of people, the possibilities of technology, and the requirements for business success. It consists of five stages: Empathize (understand users), Define (state problems), Ideate (generate solutions), Prototype (build representations), and Test (validate with users). For Product Managers, design thinking provides a structured framework for tackling complex problems, ensuring that solutions are grounded in real user needs rather than assumptions. It encourages experimentation, iteration, and cross-functional collaboration.
