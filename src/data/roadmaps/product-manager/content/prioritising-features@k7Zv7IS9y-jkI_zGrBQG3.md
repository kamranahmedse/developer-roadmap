# Prioritising Features

Feature prioritization is the process of deciding which features to build first based on their expected impact, effort, and strategic alignment. It's one of the most critical responsibilities of a Product Manager, as it directly affects the product's success and the team's effectiveness. Common prioritization frameworks include RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort), MoSCoW (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have), Kano model, and value vs effort matrices. Effective prioritization requires balancing customer needs, business goals, technical constraints, and team capacity. The best prioritization decisions are data-informed but also consider strategic context and user empathy.
