# Maturity

The maturity stage represents the peak of a product's market adoption and profitability. During this phase, sales growth slows as the product reaches market saturation. Competition is typically intense, and differentiation becomes more challenging. Product Managers focus on defending market share, optimizing operations, and finding ways to extend the product's lifecycle through innovation or market expansion. Key strategies include cost optimization, feature refinement, customer retention programs, and exploring adjacent markets. The maturity stage requires careful management to maximize profitability while preparing for potential decline.
