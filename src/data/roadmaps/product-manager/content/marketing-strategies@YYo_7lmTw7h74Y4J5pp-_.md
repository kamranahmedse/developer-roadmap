# Marketing Strategies

Marketing strategies in product management define how the product will reach, engage, and convert target customers. They encompass the channels, messaging, positioning, and tactics used to drive awareness, acquisition, and retention. Product Managers collaborate with marketing teams to develop strategies that align with product goals and customer needs. Key considerations include target audience segmentation, channel selection (content marketing, paid advertising, social media, email, partnerships), messaging frameworks, and measurement approaches. Effective marketing strategies are data-driven, customer-centric, and aligned with the overall product and business strategy.
