# Execution

Execution in product management refers to the process of turning product strategy and plans into tangible results. It encompasses the day-to-day activities of building, shipping, and iterating on products, including sprint planning, backlog management, stakeholder coordination, and quality assurance. Effective execution requires strong project management skills, the ability to make trade-off decisions, and a focus on delivering value incrementally. Product Managers must balance speed with quality, manage dependencies across teams, and ensure that the product vision is maintained throughout the development process. Successful execution is what separates great product strategies from great products.
