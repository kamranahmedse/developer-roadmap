# LTV (Lifetime Value)

LTV (Lifetime Value) or CLV (Customer Lifetime Value) is the total revenue a business can expect from a single customer account throughout their relationship. It's a critical metric for understanding the long-term value of customers and making informed decisions about acquisition spending, retention efforts, and product investment. Product Managers use LTV to calculate the LTV:CAC ratio (ideally 3:1 or higher), prioritize features that increase customer lifetime, and make strategic decisions about market segments. Increasing LTV through retention, upselling, and cross-selling is often more efficient than acquiring new customers.
