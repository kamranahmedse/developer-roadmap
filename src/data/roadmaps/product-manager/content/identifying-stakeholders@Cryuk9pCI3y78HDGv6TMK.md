# Identifying Stakeholders

Identifying stakeholders is the first step in stakeholder management. It involves mapping all individuals and groups who are affected by, can influence, or have an interest in the product. Stakeholders can be internal (executives, engineers, designers, sales, marketing, support) or external (customers, partners, regulators, investors). Product Managers use stakeholder identification to ensure that all relevant perspectives are considered in product decisions and that communication and engagement strategies are appropriately targeted. A comprehensive stakeholder map prevents surprises and ensures that no critical perspectives are overlooked.
