# Outcome-Based Roadmaps

Outcome-based roadmaps are strategic planning tools that center around goals and results rather than features and timelines. Instead of listing what will be built, they focus on the problems to solve and the outcomes to achieve. This approach helps teams stay focused on delivering value rather than just shipping features. Product Managers use outcome-based roadmaps to align teams around measurable goals, encourage experimentation, and maintain flexibility in how outcomes are achieved. The roadmap communicates desired outcomes (e.g., "increase user retention by 20%") rather than specific features, giving teams the autonomy to find the best solutions.
