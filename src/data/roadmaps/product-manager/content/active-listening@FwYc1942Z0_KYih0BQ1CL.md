# Active Listening

Active listening is a communication technique where the listener fully concentrates on, understands, responds to, and remembers what is being said. For Product Managers, active listening is essential for understanding user needs, building rapport with stakeholders, and facilitating productive team discussions. Key practices include giving full attention, asking clarifying questions, paraphrasing to confirm understanding, and withholding judgment. Active listening helps PMs uncover hidden needs, build trust, and make better-informed decisions. It's particularly valuable during user interviews, stakeholder meetings, and conflict resolution.
