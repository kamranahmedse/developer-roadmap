# Value Proposition Canvas

The Value Proposition Canvas is a strategic tool developed by Alexander Osterwalder that helps Product Managers design, test, and refine value propositions that truly resonate with customers. It consists of two sides: the Customer Profile (jobs, pains, and gains) and the Value Map (products/services, pain relievers, and gain creators). The canvas helps teams achieve fit between what the market wants and what the product offers. Product Managers use it to systematically analyze customer needs and ensure that every feature and capability directly addresses a real customer job, pain, or gain.
