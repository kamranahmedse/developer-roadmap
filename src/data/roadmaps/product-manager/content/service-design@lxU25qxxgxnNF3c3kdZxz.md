# Service Design

Service design is a holistic approach to planning and organizing people, infrastructure, and material components of a service to improve its quality and the interaction between the service provider and its customers. For Product Managers, service design thinking helps consider the entire customer journey across all touchpoints - not just the digital product, but also support, onboarding, billing, and other service elements. It uses tools like service blueprints, customer journey maps, and ecosystem maps to visualize and improve the end-to-end experience. Service design ensures that products are delivered as part of a coherent, delightful service experience.
