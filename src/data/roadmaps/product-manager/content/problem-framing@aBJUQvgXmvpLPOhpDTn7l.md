# Problem Framing

Problem framing is a critical thinking technique used by Product Managers to clearly define and understand the problem before jumping to solutions. It involves breaking down complex challenges into manageable components, identifying root causes rather than symptoms, and establishing the right context for problem-solving. Good problem framing ensures that teams are solving the right problem, not just the most obvious one. Techniques include the 5 Whys, problem statements, "How Might We" questions, and jobs-to-be-done frameworks. Effective problem framing is essential for product discovery and helps prevent wasted effort on solutions that don't address the real user need.
