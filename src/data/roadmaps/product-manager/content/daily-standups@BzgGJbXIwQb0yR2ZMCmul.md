# Daily Standups

Daily standups (also known as daily scrums) are short, time-boxed meetings (typically 15 minutes) where team members share what they accomplished yesterday, what they plan to do today, and any blockers they're facing. The purpose is to maintain transparency, identify issues early, and keep the team aligned. For Product Managers, standups provide a pulse on development progress, surface blockers that may need intervention, and help identify when priorities may need adjustment. Effective standups focus on coordination rather than status reporting and respect everyone's time.
