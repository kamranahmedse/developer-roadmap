# Statement

A product statement is a concise declaration that captures the essence of what a product is, who it serves, and why it matters. It serves as a north star for the product team, helping to align efforts and communicate the product's purpose to stakeholders. A well-crafted product statement typically includes the target audience, the problem being solved, and the unique approach or value proposition. It differs from a vision statement in that it's more concrete and actionable, focusing on the present rather than the future. Product Managers use statements to ensure clarity of purpose and to guide day-to-day decision-making.
