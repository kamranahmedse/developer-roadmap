# Target

A target in product management is a specific, measurable outcome that the product team aims to achieve within a defined timeframe. Targets translate strategic goals into concrete benchmarks that guide daily work and enable progress measurement. They can be related to user acquisition, revenue, engagement, retention, or any other key metric. Product Managers set targets based on historical data, market analysis, and strategic priorities. Well-defined targets are ambitious yet achievable, aligned with broader goals, and communicated clearly to the team. They serve as motivation and accountability mechanisms.
