# Future Constraints

Future constraints are anticipated limitations and challenges that may impact the product in the future. Product Managers must proactively identify and plan for these constraints to ensure long-term product success. Future constraints can include emerging regulations, technological shifts, changing customer expectations, competitive pressures, and resource limitations. By anticipating these constraints, teams can make strategic decisions today that position the product for success tomorrow, such as building flexible architectures, establishing partnerships, or investing in emerging capabilities.
