# Managing Stakeholders

Stakeholder management is the process of identifying, understanding, and engaging the individuals and groups who have an interest in or influence over the product. Product Managers must balance competing stakeholder needs, communicate effectively with different audiences, and build support for product decisions. Effective stakeholder management involves regular communication, setting clear expectations, involving stakeholders in appropriate decisions, and maintaining transparency about trade-offs and priorities. Strong stakeholder relationships are built on trust, respect, and consistent delivery of value.
