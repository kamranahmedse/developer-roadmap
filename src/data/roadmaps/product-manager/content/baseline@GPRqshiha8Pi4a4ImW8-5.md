# Baseline

A baseline in product management is the starting point or reference value for a metric against which future performance is measured. Establishing baselines is essential for setting realistic targets, measuring progress, and evaluating the impact of product changes. Product Managers use baselines to understand current performance, identify improvement opportunities, and track the effectiveness of product initiatives. Baselines should be established using reliable data from a representative time period and documented clearly so that all team members understand the starting point for measurement.
