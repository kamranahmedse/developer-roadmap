# Identify Partners

Identifying potential partners is a strategic activity for Product Managers that involves evaluating organizations whose capabilities, resources, or market access could complement your product. This process includes assessing partner fit (strategic alignment, cultural compatibility, capability complementarity), evaluating partnership models (integration, co-development, distribution), and building business cases for partnership initiatives. Effective partner identification requires understanding your own product's strengths and gaps, mapping the ecosystem landscape, and evaluating potential partners based on their ability to create mutual value.
