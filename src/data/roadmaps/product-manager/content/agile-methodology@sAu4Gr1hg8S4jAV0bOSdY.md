# Agile Methodology

Agile methodology is an iterative approach to software development that emphasizes flexibility, collaboration, and customer feedback. It breaks work into small, manageable increments delivered in short cycles (sprints), allowing teams to adapt quickly to changing requirements. Popular agile frameworks include Scrum (structured sprints with defined roles), Kanban (continuous flow with visual boards), and XP (extreme programming with engineering practices). For Product Managers, agile provides a framework for delivering value incrementally, gathering feedback early, and adapting the product direction based on learnings. Understanding agile principles is essential for effective collaboration with development teams.
