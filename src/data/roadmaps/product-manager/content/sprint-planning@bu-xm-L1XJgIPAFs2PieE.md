# Sprint Planning

Sprint planning is a Scrum ceremony where the team decides what work will be completed during the upcoming sprint. The Product Owner presents the highest-priority backlog items, the team discusses requirements and estimates effort, and together they commit to a sprint goal. Sprint planning ensures that the team has a clear, shared understanding of what needs to be built and why. Product Managers (often serving as Product Owners) play a critical role in sprint planning by presenting priorities, clarifying requirements, and making trade-off decisions when the team's capacity is limited.
