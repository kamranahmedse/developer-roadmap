# Retention Rate

Retention rate measures the percentage of users who continue to use a product over a specified period. It's one of the most important metrics for product health because retaining existing users is typically more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. Product Managers track retention to understand product stickiness, measure the impact of product improvements, and predict long-term revenue. Retention is often measured at different intervals (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30) to understand how quickly users drop off and where improvements are needed.
