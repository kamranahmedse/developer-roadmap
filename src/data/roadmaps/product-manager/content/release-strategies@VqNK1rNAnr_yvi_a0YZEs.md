# Release Strategies

Release strategies define how and when product updates are delivered to users. They encompass the planning, coordination, and execution of product launches, including feature rollout approaches, deployment methods, and communication plans. Product Managers choose release strategies based on factors like risk tolerance, user impact, technical constraints, and business goals. Common strategies include big bang releases, phased rollouts, feature flags, and dark launches. The right release strategy minimizes risk while maximizing the value delivered to users.
