# Job Stories

Job Stories are an alternative to user stories that focus on the situation, motivation, and expected outcome rather than the user persona. They follow the format: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]." Developed by the Intercom team, job stories help teams focus on the context and motivation behind user needs rather than making assumptions about user types. This approach can lead to better solutions because it forces teams to understand the circumstances and desired outcomes rather than jumping to solutions based on assumed user characteristics.
