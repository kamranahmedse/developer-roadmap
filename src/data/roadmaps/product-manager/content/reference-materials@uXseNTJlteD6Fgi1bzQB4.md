# Reference Materials

Reference materials in product management context include the foundational documents, research, and resources that inform product decisions. These can include market research reports, user studies, competitive analyses, industry benchmarks, and technical documentation. Maintaining a well-organized collection of reference materials ensures that product decisions are evidence-based and that teams can quickly access the context they need. Product Managers use reference materials to support strategic discussions, onboard new team members, and maintain institutional knowledge about the product and its market.
