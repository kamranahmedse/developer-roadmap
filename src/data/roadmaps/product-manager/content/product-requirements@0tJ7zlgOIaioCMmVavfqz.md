# Product Requirements

Product requirements define what a product should do and how it should behave to meet user needs and business goals. They bridge the gap between product strategy and development execution, translating high-level goals into specific, actionable specifications. Requirements can be functional (what the product does) or non-functional (how well it performs). Product Managers are responsible for gathering, documenting, and prioritizing requirements based on user research, stakeholder input, and strategic priorities. Well-defined requirements reduce ambiguity, prevent scope creep, and ensure that development teams build the right product.
