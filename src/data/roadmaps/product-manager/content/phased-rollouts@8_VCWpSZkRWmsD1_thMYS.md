# Phased Rollouts

Phased rollouts are a release strategy where new features or products are gradually released to increasing percentages of users rather than launching to everyone at once. This approach reduces risk by limiting the blast radius of potential issues, allows teams to gather feedback from early adopters, and enables data-driven decisions about broader release. Product Managers define rollout phases (e.g., 1% → 10% → 50% → 100%), success criteria for each phase, and rollback triggers. Phased rollouts are often implemented using feature flags and are particularly valuable for high-impact changes or products with large user bases.
