# North Star Metric

The North Star Metric is the single most important metric that captures the core value your product delivers to customers. It serves as a guiding light for the entire organization, aligning teams around a shared measure of success. A good North Star Metric is a leading indicator of revenue, reflects customer value, and measures the frequency and depth of product usage. Examples include "weekly active users" for social products, "bookings per guest" for marketplaces, or "messages sent" for communication tools. Product Managers define and communicate the North Star Metric to ensure all teams are working toward the same outcome.
