# Retrospectives

Retrospectives are regular team meetings held at the end of each sprint (or project phase) to reflect on what went well, what didn't, and what should be improved. They are a cornerstone of continuous improvement in agile development. The team identifies actionable improvements and commits to implementing them in the next sprint. For Product Managers, retrospectives provide valuable insights into team dynamics, process inefficiencies, and product issues that may not be visible through metrics alone. Participating in retrospectives helps PMs understand the development process better and contribute to a culture of continuous learning.
