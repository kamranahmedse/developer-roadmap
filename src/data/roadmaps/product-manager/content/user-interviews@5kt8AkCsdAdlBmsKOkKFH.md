# User Interviews

User interviews are one-on-one conversations with users or potential customers designed to understand their needs, behaviors, and experiences. They are a primary qualitative research method used by Product Managers during product discovery to uncover insights that quantitative data alone cannot reveal. Effective user interviews involve open-ended questions, active listening, and probing for deeper understanding rather than leading the conversation. Key practices include preparing an interview guide, recruiting representative participants, recording sessions for analysis, and synthesizing findings into actionable insights. User interviews help teams understand the "why" behind user behavior and identify opportunities for product improvement.
