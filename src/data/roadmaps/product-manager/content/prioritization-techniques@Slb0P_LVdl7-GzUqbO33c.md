# Prioritization Techniques

Prioritization techniques are structured methods for deciding which features, initiatives, or tasks to work on first. They help Product Managers make objective, defensible decisions about resource allocation. Common techniques include RICE scoring (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort), MoSCoW method (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have), Value vs Effort matrices, Kano model, and Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF). Each technique has its strengths and is suited to different contexts. The best prioritization approach combines quantitative scoring with qualitative judgment and strategic thinking.
