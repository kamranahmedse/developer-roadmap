# Writing PRDs

A Product Requirements Document (PRD) is a comprehensive document that outlines the purpose, features, functionality, and behavior of a product or feature. Writing effective PRDs is a core skill for Product Managers. A good PRD includes the problem statement, goals and success metrics, user stories, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, design considerations, and out-of-scope items. The PRD serves as a communication tool between product, design, and engineering teams, ensuring everyone understands what needs to be built and why. Modern PRDs are often living documents that evolve as the team learns more through development and testing.
