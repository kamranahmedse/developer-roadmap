# Stakeholder Mapping

Stakeholder mapping is a visual technique for categorizing stakeholders based on their level of interest in and influence over the product. Common frameworks include power/interest grids, influence/impact matrices, and salience models. Mapping helps Product Managers prioritize stakeholder engagement efforts, tailor communication strategies, and identify potential allies and blockers. High-influence, high-interest stakeholders require close management, while low-influence, low-interest stakeholders may need only periodic updates. Stakeholder mapping should be revisited regularly as stakeholder dynamics change over time.
