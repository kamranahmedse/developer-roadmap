# Surveys and Questionnaires

Surveys and questionnaires are quantitative research tools that allow Product Managers to gather structured feedback from a large number of users efficiently. They are used to validate hypotheses, measure satisfaction, prioritize features, and understand user preferences at scale. Effective surveys require careful question design to avoid bias, appropriate sampling to ensure representativeness, and thoughtful analysis to extract meaningful insights. Product Managers use surveys throughout the product lifecycle - from discovery (understanding needs) to post-launch (measuring satisfaction). When combined with qualitative methods like interviews, surveys provide a comprehensive understanding of user needs and behaviors.
