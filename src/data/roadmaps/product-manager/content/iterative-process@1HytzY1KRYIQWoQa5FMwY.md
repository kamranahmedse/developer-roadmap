# Iterative Process

The iterative process is a cyclical approach to product development where teams build, test, and refine a product through repeated cycles. Each iteration involves planning, designing, developing, testing, and gathering feedback, with the insights from one cycle informing the next. For Product Managers, the iterative process is fundamental to agile development and allows teams to reduce risk by validating assumptions early and often. Rather than attempting to build a perfect product in one go, iterative development embraces learning through experimentation and continuous improvement.
