# Defining Goals

Defining goals is a critical product management activity that translates product strategy into measurable outcomes. Goals provide direction, enable prioritization, and create accountability. Product Managers use frameworks like OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) to set goals that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Well-defined goals align teams around shared outcomes, help stakeholders understand what success looks like, and provide a basis for measuring progress. Goals should cascade from company-level objectives to product-level targets, ensuring that product work contributes to broader business outcomes.
